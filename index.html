One of my hobbies is to do 3D art and simulations. My preferred software is called Houdini. It is a paid program unlike Blender, but it has a lot of cool features. My favorite feature is its built-in coding language, vex. It allows you to use equations or references to replace numbers during animations.

These nine images are from tutorials I've followed. The first five are from SideFX's in-house tutorials. The other four are from a series called "Stop being afraid of Houdini", by Mark Fancher.
